title: Ads (Beta)
description: Partial LinkedIn, Facebook/Instagram, Google, and TikTok ads modules — not part of the unified publishing API.
---

Postsiva includes **partial** ads tooling under platform-native `/linkedin-ads`, `/tiktok-ads`, and related routes. This is separate from organic unified publishing.

## Status

| Network | Status |
|---------|--------|
| LinkedIn Ads | Partial (accounts, text campaigns / creatives) |
| TikTok Ads | Partial (auth, advertiser info) |
| Facebook / Google Ads | Partial modules in backend |

## Important

- Ads are **not** exposed on Unified MCP today
- Prefer the web **Ad platform** UI where available
- Organic posts remain under `/unified/post/*`

<Warning>
  Ads APIs may change without the same stability guarantees as unified publishing. Confirm with `GET /docs` (Swagger) on your environment before building production ads automations.
</Warning>
