Chat with your workspace over **WhatsApp** instead of the web agent or MCP.

## Enable

1. Upgrade to **Pro** (`whatsapp_agent_enabled`)
2. Open **Settings → WhatsApp** in the app
3. Link your phone / complete provider setup
4. Message the Business number

## What you can ask

Same tool family as [MCP tools](/mcp/tools):

- “What accounts are connected?”
- “Post this to LinkedIn and Threads…”
- “Show my scheduled posts”
- “Reply to this comment…”
- “Turn this idea into a LinkedIn post”

## Automations

- Prefer **REST + API key** or **MCP** for deterministic pipelines
- Use WhatsApp for conversational control on mobile
